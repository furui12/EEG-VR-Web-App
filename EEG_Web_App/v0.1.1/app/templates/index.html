How a session works

  1. Participant — pick or register who is taking part.
  2. Connect streams — start the AURA software and the VR game; wait until EEG (and VR) turn green.
  3. Signal & record — look at the live signal, choose filters, then Start. Press Stop when the task ends.
  4. Saved — the trial is saved automatically as an .xdf file. Add notes and record the next trial.

Status lights (top right)

  • receiving data
  • found but not connected yet / checking
  • not found

Where are the files?

In the data/ folder of the app (shown after each trial) and in History, where you can download them.
